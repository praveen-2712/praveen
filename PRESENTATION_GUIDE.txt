                    NEUROLOGIX PRO V3 — THE COMPLETE TECHNICAL GUIDE

"Precision. Explainability. Reliability. Clinical-Grade Diagnostic AI."

This guide provides a comprehensive breakdown of the Neurologix Pro V3 system. 
It is designed for beginners to understand every technical decision, value, 
and architectural choice made in this clinical-grade brain tumor MRI pipeline.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PROJECT MISSION & PROBLEM DEFINITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Brain tumor diagnosis demands more than just "Accuracy." It requires:
1. Spatial Precision: Exactly where is the tumor?
2. Explainability: Why did the AI make this decision?
3. Consensus: Do different models (classification vs detection) agree?

Neurologix Pro V3 solves this using a 3-model ensemble and a clinical 
inference engine.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DATA: THE FOUNDATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We use two primary data sources to avoid "domain shift" and ensure robustness:

[A] The BraTS 2020 Dataset (Segmentation & Detection)
    - Source: 4.16 GB of NIfTI (.nii.gz) volumetric data.
    - MODALITY: FLAIR (Fluid-Attenuated Inversion Recovery).
    - PREP: We extract 10,364 axial slices where the tumor is present.
    - ROLE: Used to train the U-Net (Pixel-level) and YOLOv8m (Bounding boxes).

[B] The Kaggle MRI Dataset (Classification)
    - SOURCE: ~7,000 images across 4 classes.
    - CLASSES: Glioma, Meningioma, No Tumor, Pituitary.
    - ROLE: Used to train the EfficientNet-B4 "Diagnostic Head."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE PREPROCESSING PIPELINE (preprocess.py)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Medical images vary wildly across scanners (Siemens vs GE). We standardize them:

[A] Anatomical Brain Masking
    - We use a thresholding and contour-finding algorithm to isolate the brain.
    - WHY: This prevents the AI from looking at the skull or scanner text.

[B] Histogram Matching (Golden Reference)
    - Every input scan's brightness distribution is "matched" to a pre-selected
      "Golden Reference" scan from the training set.
    - WHY: Standardizes contrast across different field strengths (1.5T vs 3T).

[C] Triple-Stream CLAHE (dataset.py)
    - Each grayscale MRI slice is processed with three different CLAHE 
      (Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization) levels:
      - Channel R: Clip Limit 1.5
      - Channel G: Clip Limit 2.0
      - Channel B: Clip Limit 2.5
    - WHY: This converts a flat grayscale image into a high-contrast RGB 
      tensor, highlighting subtle tumor textures that a human might miss.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. MODEL 1: THE DIAGNOSTIC HEAD (EfficientNet-B4)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- ARCHITECTURE: EfficientNet-B4 (compound-scaled for resolution/depth).
- INPUT SIZE: 380x380 pixels (captures fine anatomical detail).
- TRAINING TRICKS:
    - Differential LR: The backbone (features) trains at 2e-5 (slow), 
      while the head (classifier) trains at 1e-4 (fast).
    - OneCycleLR: A learning rate that "warms up" and then "cools down" per 
      batch, providing faster and more stable convergence.
    - Label Smoothing (0.05): Prevents the model from becoming "too certain" 
      about noisy labels, improving generalizability.

[STOCHASTIC INFERENCE]
- Neurologix doesn't run 1 pass. It runs 19 PASSES per image:
    - 15 MC-Dropout Passes: Randomly drops neural connections to measure 
      "Model Uncertainty."
    - 4 TTA Views: Flips and rotates the image (Horizontal, Vertical, etc.).
- THE RESULT: A robust average probability. If the models disagree, 
  uncertainty increases, flagging the scan for review.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. MODEL 2: THE SPATIAL LOCALIZER (YOLOv8m)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- TASK: Object Detection (Bounding Boxes).
- TRAINING: Two-phase curriculum.
    - Phase 1 (25 Epochs): Frozen backbone — trains only the detection head.
    - Phase 2 (75 Epochs): Full fine-tuning — adjusts the entire 26M parameters.
- ROLE: Provides real-time coordinates (x, y, w, h) for the tumor region.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. MODEL 3: THE PIXEL-PRECISE SEGMENTOR (ResNet50-Unet)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- ARCHITECTURE: U-Net (Encoder-Decoder) with a ResNet50 backbone.
- LOSS FUNCTION: 0.5 * DiceLoss + 0.5 * BCE.
    - Dice Score: Measures the overlap between the AI's mask and reality.
- POST-PROCESSING:
    - Connected Component Filtering: Removes tiny "noise" fragments. Only 
      components larger than 15% of the primary mass are kept.
- ROLE: Calculates Tumor Area (px) and Centroid (the exact center).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. THE CLINICAL INFERENCE ENGINE (The Orchestrator)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The "Brain" of the system that coordinates the three models:

[A] Consensus Arbitration
    - If the Classifier says "No Tumor" but YOLO detects a box with 
      confidence > 0.40, the system triggers a Safety Override: 
      "Unclassified Finding — Review Required."

[B] Cross-Modal Spatial Validation
    - It calculates the distance between the YOLO center and the U-Net center.
    - If they are > 150 pixels apart, it flags a "Discordant Modalities" alert. 
      (Meaning the models are looking at different things — a safety risk).

[C] Domain-Shift Fallback
    - If the U-Net fails to find a mask (common on noisy external data), 
      it falls back to Grad-CAM++ (Heatmap) to identify the tumor region.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. EVALUATION & RESULTS (The Numbers)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We use strict metrics that are never hardcoded:

1. Accuracy (94.69%): How many cases were correctly classified.
2. Macro F1 (0.9458): Balanced performance across all 4 classes.
3. ROC AUC (0.9881): The "Diagnostic Power." 1.0 is perfect; 0.988 is 
   state-of-the-art. It measures how well the AI separates classes.
4. Mean Dice (0.8330): The precision of the segmentation mask boundaries.
5. mAP@0.5 (0.9621): The accuracy of the YOLO bounding box localization.

[THE DEPLOYMENT GATE]
The script `evaluate.py` will CRASH if accuracy is < 91%. This prevents 
bad models from ever being used in the dashboard.
