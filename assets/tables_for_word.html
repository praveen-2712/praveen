TABLE I

LITERATURE SURVEY

Title Authors Description Research Gap
DL for Brain Tumor Diagnosis G. S. Tandel et al. (2019) Comprehensive review of CNN architectures for multi-class brain tumor classification. High variance in cross-dataset performance and lack of clinical-grade ensembles.
Transfer Learning in Neuro-Oncology S. Deepak and P. M. Ameer (2019) Evaluation of pretrained CNNs (GoogleNet) for 3-class classification on MRI. Limited spatial interpretability; no pixel-level segmentation integration.
Integrated Segmentation & Classification J. Amin et al. (2018) Proposed sequential pipeline for tumor localization and class prediction. Lacks real-time uncertainty quantification and multi-model consensus.
EfficientNet in Medical Imaging M. Tan, Q. V. Le (ICML 2019) Compound scaling for high-resolution anatomical detail in pathology. Requires domain adaptation for MRI field-strength variations.
U-Net: Biomedical Segmentation O. Ronneberger et al. (MICCAI 2015) Encoder-decoder with skip connections; standard for glioma masking. Usually operates in isolation without classification gating.
Grad-CAM++ Explainability A. Chattopadhay et al. (WACV 2018) Weighted second-order gradients for precise localization. Background noise in MRI often causes false-positive activations.
YOLO for Lesion Detection G. Jocher et al. (2023) SOTA object detection; adapted for MRI lesion localization. High sensitivity to domain shift between T1 and FLAIR sequences.
BraTS 2021 Benchmark S. Bakas et al. (arXiv 2021) Standard dataset for glioma segmentation and volumetric analysis. Most models are not integrated into diagnostic web applications.
MC-Dropout for Clinical Risk Y. Gal, Z. Ghahramani (ICML 2016) Bayesian approximation for quantifying diagnostic uncertainty. Computational overhead in real-time clinical inference environments.
Ensemble Consensus Systems M. S. Nawaz et al. (2020) Multi-class brain tumor classification using deep learning and image processing. Lacks programmatic deployment gating and centroid-based spatial validation.
